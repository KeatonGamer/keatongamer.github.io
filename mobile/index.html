Welcome to my website!

Hi, I'm Keaton. I'm an indie game developer and artist, who likes old games and software.

I discovered NeoCities in 2023, and it's (kind of?) a weird story. If you know MattKC, he talks about a lot of hardware and Lego Island. But in one of his videos, he had a sponsor. In it, the sponsor had a short segment of a website that he created, which of course, looked like a GeoCities page straight out of the 90's. You can find it right here, by the way.

I was, of course, intrigued. So I started searching the World Wide Web on where I could find this. At first, I tried Github, which actually became my backend server, I tried Carrd, Tumblr, etc, until I saw Michael MJD's video about Microsoft FrontPage, which in that video he used NeoCities for his website. Shortly enough, I realized NeoCities is exactly what I'm looking for and began building my website. Which, at the time, looked like this.

And that's how I found out about NeoCities. If you want to go back to the past, you can - just click here. (Work in progress, by the way.)
